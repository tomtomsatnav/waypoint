# Concept art credits

All paintings are in the public domain (the artists died more than 100 years ago); images from Wikimedia Commons.
They set mood, light and palette; translate them into the game's character-drawn world, do not copy them.

| File | Painting | Area |
|---|---|---|
| abbey.jpg | Caspar David Friedrich, *The Abbey in the Oakwood* (1809-10) | graveyard and ruined abbey (the start) |
| isle.jpg | Arnold Böcklin, *Isle of the Dead*, third version (1883) | the lake and the isle of tombs |
| monk.jpg | Caspar David Friedrich, *The Monk by the Sea* (1808-10) | the grey shore |
| wanderer.jpg | Caspar David Friedrich, *Wanderer above the Sea of Fog* (c. 1818) | the fog-filled crags |
| fortress.jpg | John Martin, *The Bard* (c. 1817) | the fortress over the gorge and waterfall |
| ruins.jpg | Thomas Cole, *The Course of Empire: Desolation* (1836) | the ruined city by moonlit water |
| depths.jpg | John Martin, *Pandemonium* (1841) | the molten depths |

`../look-*.png` are rough static mock-ups of the character-grid style made from these paintings (and, in
`look-depths-hud.png`, a sketch of the HUD). The game should look better than them, and move.

## Look target (used with permission)

`../look-target-*.png` are frames from a work-in-progress video by **@ansenjeo** (Instagram), "Making the ASCII MMORPG of my
dreams", used here with the creator's permission as references for the look only:

| File | Shows |
|---|---|
| look-target-wide.png | the overall look from the overhead camera: grass, a lake reflecting the moon, trees, all in dense characters |
| look-target-ripples.png | ripple rings spreading through water, drawn as rings of characters |
| look-target-grass.png | grass as thousands of fine character strokes with small flowers, and the player model drawn in characters |
| look-target-reflection.png | the player and the world reflected in still water |

| look-target-boss.png | a party fighting a huge dragon drawn in characters, with the boss bar along the top |
| look-target-boss-telegraphs.png | the dragon's attack telegraphed as dotted circles on the ground, party nameplates, the hotbar |
| look-target-party.png | party frames on the left (portrait, health, mana, level), the 1-0 ability hotbar, nameplates |
| look-target-boss-phase.png | the boss bar naming the current phase, a fallen party member greyed out, rain over dark grass |

The last four come from a second video by the same creator, "Party with friends", also used with permission.

`../look-target-v1-NN.jpg` (6 frames, "Water reflection test") and `../look-target-v2-NN.jpg` (40 frames, "Party with
friends") are further frames from the two videos, one every two seconds with near-duplicates removed, in order: v2 follows a
whole dragon fight from start to the death screen. Same creator, same permission, same use: references for the look and
play only.

`../look-target-v3-NN.jpg` are frames from a third video by the same creator ("Furry spider"), same permission and use: a
giant spider boss drawn in characters close up (fur texture, legs, webs), its boss bar, a party fighting it, and the death and
revive, in order.

They show the technique and the feel to aim for. Do not copy that project's names, characters, designs or content:
Gloamreach is its own game.

## Art style (used with permission)

`../style-*.jpg` (200 frames) are from seven work-in-progress videos by **@thirteen_autumns** (Instagram), used here with the
creator's permission as the reference for Gloamreach's art style: pixel art rendered from a 3D world. Two frames a second,
near-duplicates and end cards removed, in order, so consecutive frames also show how things move (the cape, the fog, the
sand, the ripples):

| Files | Video | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| style-explore-* | "Just us, exploring the world together" | autumn forest, cliffs and stairs, a coast, two players |
| style-cape-orb-* | "Really happy with how the cape moves now" | cape motion, a moody blue-grey scene with a giant orb |
| style-sand-* | "the sand softly shifting beneath your feet" | footprints in warm desert sand at sunset |
| style-night-* | "new personal best" | a dark night scene |
| style-fog-* | "the fog gently swirls around you as you walk through it" | fog parting around the player in ruins |
| style-pond-* | "a little forest pond and about a thousand tiny details" | ripples, lily pads, fireflies, dense foliage |
| style-trees-* | "making trees feel massive without blocking everything" | huge trees turning see-through over the player |

They show the style to aim for. Do not copy that project's names, characters, designs or content.
