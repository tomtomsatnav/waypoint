# Reference art

The images in this folder are stills from a video by @templars.oath (Instagram), used with the creator's permission as
references for the **art style only**. They show another game in development. Nothing from it (emblems, characters,
buildings as drawn, interface, words) is to be reproduced; captions were cropped out and the small watermark is to be
ignored.

| File | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `sprites-figures-closeup.jpg` | the size, proportions and finish of people; a stone building with a lit window |
| `crowd-sprites-in-wheat.jpg` | how figures sit in tall crops and read at a distance |
| `fields-*.jpg` | wheat (standing, trampled), field edges, hedges, a farmhouse, mud |
| `country-summer-road-and-stream.jpg` | open country: a track, a stream, scrub, single trees |
| `country-winter-snow.jpg` | the winter palette |
| `village-summer-*.jpg`, `village-square-and-smithy.jpg` | a village among pines: thatch and timber, plots, a market cross, a smithy's glow |
| `village-autumn-*.jpg` | the autumn palette; small floating numbers and icons over the world |
| `portrait-helm-*.jpg` | the close-up technique for story moments: large pixels, narrow warm palette, deep shadow on black |
| `ui-panel-*.jpg` | interface mood: dark slate panels, thin rules, small capitals, one accent colour |
